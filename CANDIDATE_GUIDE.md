# Candidate Guide - How to Use PsychoMetrics

## For Candidates Taking Tests

### Step 1: Register as Candidate

1. Go to the landing page
2. Click **"Candidate Login"** or go to `/candidate/register`
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - Company ID (provided by your employer)
   - Email
   - First Name & Last Name
   - Phone (optional)
   - Password
4. Click **"Register"**

### Step 2: Login

1. Go to `/candidate/login`
2. Enter your email and password
3. Optionally enter Company ID
4. Click **"Login"**

### Step 3: View Available Tests

1. After login, you'll see your **Candidate Dashboard**
2. All available tests for your company will be displayed
3. Each test shows:
   - Test title and description
   - Duration
   - Number of questions
   - Remaining attempts

### Step 4: Start a Test

1. Click **"Start Test"** on any available test
2. Read the instructions carefully
3. Click to begin when ready

### Step 5: Take the Test

**Important Rules:**
- ⚠️ **Do NOT switch tabs** - Tab switches are monitored
- ⚠️ **Do NOT copy/paste** - Copy-paste is disabled and monitored
- ⚠️ **Do NOT right-click** - Context menu is disabled
- ⚠️ **Do NOT open Developer Tools** - This will be flagged
- ⚠️ **Stay on the test page** - Any suspicious activity is recorded

**Features:**
- Timer shows remaining time
- Progress bar shows completion
- Question navigation at the top
- Answers are auto-saved
- You can go back to previous questions

### Step 6: Submit Test

1. Review all answers
2. Click **"Submit Test"** when ready
3. Confirm submission
4. View your results immediately

### Step 7: View Results

After submission, you'll see:
- Overall score and percentage
- Percentile ranking
- Category breakdown
- AI interpretation
- Radar chart visualization
- Option to download PDF report

## Security Features

The system monitors:
- ✅ Tab switches
- ✅ Copy/Paste attempts
- ✅ Right-click attempts
- ✅ Developer tools access
- ✅ Window resizing
- ✅ Screenshot attempts
- ✅ IP address and user agent

All suspicious activities are logged and included in reports.

## Troubleshooting

**Can't see any tests?**
- Contact your company administrator
- Verify your company ID is correct

**Test won't start?**
- Check if you've reached maximum attempts
- Verify the test is active

**Login issues?**
- Verify your email and password
- Check your company ID
- Contact support if needed

## Need Help?

Contact your company's HR or test administrator for assistance.
